# Student User Guide — Senjuku Nihongo (せんじゅく日本語)

Welcome to **Senjuku Nihongo**, a Japanese reading gym and Kana-writing web application (Lessons 1–25) — designed as a classroom teaching companion with a 20-minute daily drill to accelerate classical learning.

This guide is written from a **student's perspective** to walk you through registration, choosing between Learning and Quiz modes, understanding how scoring and SRS work, and climbing the Leaderboard.

---

## 1. Getting Started & Registration

When you visit Senjuku Nihongo without logging in (**Guest Mode**), you can try basic practice on **Lessons 1–5** (*Tebak Bacaan MCQ*, *Vocab Booster*, *Flashcard*, and *Tulis Kana*). To unlock all **25 Lessons**, all drill modes, **Audio Transcript**, **My Progress**, **AI Study Advisor**, and the **Leaderboard**, create a free student account.

### How to Register (Free)
1. Click **Daftar (Register)** at the top-right corner (or **✨ Buat Akun Gratis** on the guest banner).
2. Enter a public **Nickname** (2–24 characters — displayed on the Leaderboard).
3. Enter your active **Email Address** *(kept strictly private — never shown to other users)*.
4. Click **Kirim Link Verifikasi** and open the single-use activation link sent to your email *(valid for 24 hours)*.
5. Set your **Password** (minimum 6 characters) to activate your account. You will be logged in automatically.

### Profile & Account Security
- Click your **Avatar & Nickname** pill at the top-right to open **Profile**.
- You can update your **Nickname**, choose an **Avatar Emoji**, add a short **Bio**, or change your password under **🔑 Ubah Kata Sandi (Change Password)**.
- Forgot your password? Click **Lupa kata sandi?** in the Login modal to receive a password reset link via email *(up to 2 requests per 24 hours)*.

## 2. Selecting Lessons & the 5-Grade Unlock System

Before starting a drill in **Daily Drill**, choose your study material under **Pilih Bab (Select Lessons)**:
- Select one lesson, multiple lessons, or use quick presets (`1-5`, `6-10`, `Semua`, etc.). At least **1 Lesson** must be selected to enable the **▶ START** button.
- **5-Block Gradual Unlock Progression**:
  - **Grade 1 (Lessons 1–5)** is unlocked from day one.
  - **Grade 2 (Lessons 6–10)** &rarr; **Grade 3 (Lessons 11–15)** &rarr; **Grade 4 (Lessons 16–20)** &rarr; **Grade 5 (Lessons 21–25)** unlock progressively as you master the lessons in your current block.

## 3. Two Main Options: Learning Mode vs Quiz Mode

On the **Daily Drill** screen, you always choose between two practice modes:

| Aspect | 💡 Learning Mode | ⚡ Quiz Mode (Reflex Test) |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | Relaxed reading, listening, and memorization | Testing reading/typing reflexes and active recall |
| **Time Pressure** | Relaxed (no strict scoring pressure) | Timed & fast-paced |
| **Romaji & Translation** | Available to support comprehension | Restricted/hidden depending on question type to train real recall |
| **Scoring & SRS Impact** | Builds initial familiarity | Calculates **Session Score (0–100)**, **Combo XP**, and promotes/demotes **SRS** cards |

## 4. Walkthrough of Every Learning & Quiz Type

### A. Mode Latihan (Learning Mode)
1. **Flashcard**
   - Shows vocabulary or Kanji on the front. Press **Space** or click the card to flip and reveal the reading, Romaji, meaning, and example sentence.
   - Click the audio icon (or press `R`) to play native Tokyo pronunciation.
2. **Tulis Cepat**
   - **Word Typing**: Practice typing Kana/Kanji readings at your own pace using the built-in Kana Keyboard.
   - **Full Sentence Typing**: Practice transcribing full audio sentences calmly without timer stress.
3. **Vocab Booster** *(cascading: **Vocab Booster: Kana** & **Vocab Booster: Kanji**)*
   - Match **3 Indonesian meanings** on the left with **3 Kana or Kanji cards** on the right, then submit all 3 pairs at once (`1× submit`).
4. **Speak Aloud**
   - Practice speaking 5 words and 3 short sentences aloud with `Play Normal` / `Play Slow (0.7×)` audio cues (`+3 XP` Word / `+5 XP` Sentence warm-up, defaulting to **Romaji: OFF**, **Meaning: ON**).

### B. Mode Quiz (8 Questions per Session)
Every Quiz Mode session consists of **8 bite-sized questions** to keep your focus sharp:

1. **Pilihan Ganda** *(cascading: **Tebak Baca** & **Kalimat Rumpang**)*
   - **Tebak Baca**: Choose 1 of 4 reading options (keyboard shortcuts `1`–`4`). Option positions are uniformly randomized on every question.
   - **Kalimat Rumpang**: Fill in the missing word or particle inside a complete sentence with visual context cues. Requires **100% accuracy** for `100 🏆` and awards **2× weight**.
2. **Ketik Cepat** *(cascading: **Kosa Kata** & **Kalimat Penuh**)*
   - **Kosa Kata**: Type the Kana reading directly using the built-in *Kana On-Screen Keyboard*.
   - **Kalimat Penuh**: Listen to native audio (up to **2× plays**) and type the full sentence in Kana within **50 seconds**.
3. **Vocab Booster** *(cascading: **Vocab Booster: Kana** & **Vocab Booster: Kanji**)*
   - Match 3 meanings with 3 Kana (or Kanji) cards on a single screen. Cards are strictly shuffled so no pair starts pre-aligned.
4. **Perbaiki Skor Saya**
   - Automatically gathers words and sentences you answered incorrectly in the **past 7 days** for targeted repetition.
5. **Spesial Katakana**
   - Fast-paced reading drill focused specifically on foreign loanwords in Katakana.
6. **Speak Aloud** *(Highest Score Multiplier — 2.5× Word / 3.5× Sentence)*
   - 8-question session (`5` words + `3` short sentences, 50/50 random Kana/Kanji). Tap **Tekan & Ucapkan** to read aloud via microphone (`25s` timer for words with `2.5×` multiplier / `+25 XP` base, `50s` for sentences with `3.5×` multiplier / `+35 XP` base — highest score per question).

### Quick Keyboard Shortcuts
- `1`–`4` : Select a Multiple Choice (MCQ) option
- `Enter` : Submit answer / move to the next question
- `Space` : Flip Flashcard / play audio
- `R` : Replay audio
- `M` : Toggle Romaji visibility (when available in the active mode)

## 6. How Scoring, XP & SRS Work

### 1. Session Score (`0 – 100`)
- Every Quiz Mode session calculates a **Session Score** from your correct answers out of 8 questions:
  $$\text{Session Score} = \text{round}\left(\frac{\text{Correct Answers}}{\text{Total Questions}} \times 100\right)$$
  Example: 6 correct out of 8 questions = **75 / 100**.

### 2. Early Exit Penalty
- If you click **Keluar (Exit)** before finishing all 8 questions, all remaining unanswered questions are counted as **wrong**.
- This lowers your session score and demotes those skipped cards in SRS. Always finish all 8 questions whenever possible!

### 3. XP, Combo & Levels
- Every correct answer earns **XP**. Consecutive correct answers build a **Combo** multiplier for bonus XP.
- **Kalimat Rumpang (Cloze)** carries **2× weight** for coverage progress.
- **Speak Aloud** awards the highest multipliers & XP: **Speak Aloud (Word)** = **2.5×** (`+25 XP` base), and **Speak Aloud (Sentence)** = **3.5×** (`+35 XP` base — highest score per question).
- Accumulating XP raises your **Level** and unlocks achievement **Badges**.

### 4. Spaced Repetition System (SRS — Leitner Boxes)
- **SRS (*Spaced Repetition System*)** repeats vocabulary right before you are about to forget it: harder cards appear more often, while mastered cards appear less frequently.
- Each vocabulary item/sentence tracks a mastery **Stage (`0 – 4`)**:
- **Correct Answer**: Promotes the card by 1 stage with longer review intervals (**1 → 2 → 4 → 7 → 14 days**). Cards at **Stage ≥ 3** count as *mastered cards* on the Leaderboard.
- **Wrong Answer**: Resets the card's streak to 0, demotes it by 1 stage, makes it immediately due again, and adds it to **Perbaiki Skor Saya**.

### 5. Total Score (`/ 2,500`) & Lesson Coverage Score (`0 – 100`)
- Each lesson (Lessons 1–25) has a mastery score from `0–100`, giving a **maximum Total Score of 2,500**.
- **Per-Card Credit**:
  - **Quiz Mode (correct answer)** = `100%` credit for that card.
  - **Learning Mode (viewing a card)** = `10%` warm-up credit (upgrades to `100%` once answered correctly in Quiz Mode).
  - **Wrong answers** never reduce credit you already earned.
- **Question Weights & `100 🏆` Requirement**:
  - **Vocab/Kanji** and **Sentence Romaji Typing** carry **1×** weight, **Kalimat Rumpang (Cloze)** carries **2×** weight, **Speak Aloud (Word)** carries **2.5×** weight, and **Speak Aloud (Sentence)** carries **3.5×** weight (highest score per question).
  - Earning **`100 🏆`** on a lesson requires **≥ 80% total weighted coverage** **and** **100% of Kalimat Rumpang** answered correctly in Quiz Mode (otherwise the lesson score is capped at **89**).
- **Repeating Quizzes**: Answering new cards correctly increases **Total Score**. Repeating cards already at `100%` still increases **XP, Level, Weekly Minutes, and SRS strength**, without inflating Total Score.

## 7. How the Leaderboard Works

The **位 Papan Peringkat (Leaderboard)** lets you track your standing alongside other verified learners:

- **Ranking Criteria** *(applied to both **All-Time** and **Weekly**)*:
  1. **Total Score (`/ 2,500`)** as the primary rank.
  2. **Total XP** as the first tie-breaker.
  3. **Weekly Study Minutes** as the next tie-breaker (followed by *Daily Streak* and *Mastered Cards*).
- **Period Filters & Score Guide**: Switch between **Sepanjang Waktu** and **Minggu Ini**, and click **ℹ️ Cara Hitung Skor** directly on the page for a quick breakdown.
- **100% Privacy-Safe**:
  - The Leaderboard **only** displays *Nickname*, *Avatar*, *Total Score*, *Level*, *XP*, *Streak*, *Mastered Cards*, *Weekly Minutes*, and *Badges*.
  - Your **email address**, detailed mistake history, and private records are **never** exposed.
